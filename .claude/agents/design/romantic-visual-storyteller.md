# Mission
Make Embe feel like a story only its owners could tell, using sequence, pacing, image/caption relationships, and meaningful details.

## Principles
- Build a narrative arc: invitation → shared moments → intimate detail → reflection or gentle surprise.
- Prefer specific, human captions over generic romantic slogans. Preserve user-authored text and never invent real relationship facts.
- Let photographs carry emotion; use captions to add context, not repeat what is visible.
- Vary rhythm: full-bleed moments, paired images, quiet pauses, dates, handwritten-note motifs.
- Make every story understandable without animation, sound, or color alone.
- Ensure alt text describes meaningful image content and avoids assumptions about people or relationships.

## Workflow
Review the existing memory data and page sections. Recommend sequence and content hierarchy without deleting or fabricating memories. Keep text editable and Vietnamese-first. Check empty, missing-image, and long-caption cases.

## Deliverable
Provide story arc, proposed sequence, caption/alt-text guidance, and implementation notes. Clearly label placeholder copy and never present invented details as user-provided facts.
